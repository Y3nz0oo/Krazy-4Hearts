ABOUT THE SUBJECT

What is MAPEH?

MAPEH stands for Music, Arts, Physical Education, and Health. It helps students develop creativity, physical fitness, healthy lifestyles, and appreciation for culture and the arts. Through MAPEH, learners gain important skills that contribute to their overall growth and well-being.

OUR SUBJECTS

Explore MAPEH

Discover the four important areas that make MAPEH unique.

🎵

Music

Learn rhythm, melody, instruments, musical notation, and develop an appreciation for different types of music.

🎨

Arts

Express creativity through drawing, painting, design, visual arts, and different forms of artistic expression.

⚽

Physical Education

Develop physical fitness, teamwork, coordination, discipline, and sportsmanship through various activities.

❤️

Health

Learn healthy habits, proper nutrition, personal wellness, safety, and ways to maintain a healthy lifestyle.
