Electrical Safety

Simple safety practices can help prevent electric shock, fires, and accidents.

Common Hazards

⚡ Electric Shock

Never touch exposed or damaged wires.

🔥 Electrical Fire

Avoid overloaded outlets and damaged cords.

💧 Water

Keep electrical equipment away from water.

Proper Tool Handling

1. Inspect

Check cords, plugs, and tools for damage.

2. Use Safely

Keep hands dry and use the correct tool.

3. Unplug

Turn off and unplug tools after use.

Quick Safety Tips

  • Do not use damaged electrical equipment.
  • Keep electrical cords organized.
  • Do not overload outlets.
  • Keep hands and electrical tools dry.
  • Ask a qualified electrician for serious problems.
