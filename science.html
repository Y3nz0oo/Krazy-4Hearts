Reaction Rates in Everyday Life

How different factors affect chemical reactions around us.
01

Food Preservation

Temperature & Activation Energy

Lowering the temperature in refrigerators reduces the kinetic energy of particles, which significantly slows down bacterial growth and oxidation reactions that cause food to spoil (LibreTexts, 2023).

"The decrease in temperature decreases the rate at which food will break down or be broken down by bacteria..." — LibreTexts, 2023
02

Material Production

Catalysts & Gas Pressure

Industrial manufacturing relies heavily on catalysts and pressure manipulation to maximize production efficiency. In processes like the Haber process for synthesizing fertilizers, catalysts lower activation energy while high gas pressure forces a higher frequency of successful particle collisions (Save My Exams, 2022).

03

Fire Control

Surface Area & Concentration

Fire suppression works by targeting surface area and reactant availability. Large logs burn slowly, whereas fine wood dust presents a high surface-area-to-volume ratio that can cause explosive combustion; smothering a fire limits oxygen concentration, effectively stopping the oxidation reaction (National Institute of Standards and Technology, 2021).

04

Pollution Prevention

Heterogeneous Catalysts

Automotive catalytic converters utilize precious metals like platinum and rhodium to accelerate redox reactions. These catalysts adsorb toxic pollutants like nitrogen oxides and carbon monoxide, providing an alternative pathway with lower activation energy to convert them into harmless gases (The Open University, 2021).

05

Corrosion Control

Phase Interfaces & Separation

The corrosion of metal requires simultaneous exposure to moisture and oxygen. Applying protective barrier coatings like paint or zinc galvanization prevents reactants from contacting the metal interface, driving the rate of the oxidation reaction down to zero (AMPP, 2021).
